================================================================================
HURST CYCLIC TRADING - ALPHA VALIDATION ROADMAP
Professional Quantitative Testing Plan (8-Week Sprint)
================================================================================

WEEK 1: BASELINES & SIGNIFICANCE TESTING
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
├── Baseline Establishment
│   ├── Buy-and-Hold Benchmark (SPY)
│   ├── Random Entry Signals
│   └── SMA Crossover Baseline
├── Statistical Significance
│   ├── Binomial Test (Win Rate)
│   ├── t-Test (Returns)
│   └── Confidence Intervals (95%)
└── Result: Is alpha statistically significant? (p < 0.05?)

WEEK 2: ROBUSTNESS TESTING
├── Walk-Forward Validation
│   ├── 10 periods with expanding train window
│   ├── Verify performance consistency
│   └── Check for curve-fitting
├── Parameter Sensitivity
│   ├── Test confluence thresholds (20%-50%)
│   ├── Test risk per trade (1%-3%)
│   └── Create sensitivity heatmap
└── Equity Curve Stability
    ├── Consecutive wins/losses analysis
    ├── Single trade impact assessment
    └── Result: Is edge robust or lucky?

WEEK 3-4: RISK ANALYSIS
├── Drawdown Analysis
│   ├── Maximum Drawdown
│   ├── Recovery Time
│   └── Drawdown Frequency
├── Value at Risk (VaR/CVaR)
│   ├── 5% worst-case loss
│   └── Expected Shortfall
├── Risk-Adjusted Metrics
│   ├── Sharpe Ratio (target > 1.5)
│   ├── Sortino Ratio (target > 1.5)
│   └── Calmar Ratio (Return/MaxDD)
└── Result: Is risk acceptable?

WEEK 4: MARKET REGIME ANALYSIS
├── Trending vs Mean-Reverting
│   ├── Uptrend performance
│   ├── Downtrend performance
│   └── Sideways performance (BEST)
├── Volatility Regime Testing
│   ├── Low Vol (VIX < 15)
│   ├── Medium Vol (VIX 15-25)
│   └── High Vol (VIX > 25)
└── Multi-Asset Consistency
    ├── SPY, QQQ, IWM (equities)
    ├── GLD (commodities)
    ├── TLT (bonds)
    └── Result: Works on 70%+ of assets?

WEEK 5: EDGE DECOMPOSITION
├── Ablation Testing (Remove each feature)
│   ├── Without FLD signals
│   ├── Without spectral signatures
│   ├── Without phase analysis
│   └── Without psychological barriers
├── Cycle Profitability Analysis
│   ├── Which cycles make money?
│   └── 18-month, 40-week, 20-week, etc.
└── Result: Which components create alpha?

WEEK 5-6: REAL-WORLD CONSTRAINTS
├── Liquidity Analysis
│   ├── Position size vs daily volume
│   ├── Estimated slippage
│   └── Can execute all trades?
├── Overnight Gap Analysis
│   ├── How often gaps hurt us?
│   └── Max gap loss observed
└── Market Hours Constraint
    └── Can execute during regular hours?

WEEK 6-7: FORWARD TESTING
├── Out-of-Sample Testing
│   ├── Train on 80% of data
│   ├── Test on unseen 20%
│   └── Does performance match?
├── Paper Trading Simulation
│   ├── 3-6 months simulated trading
│   └── Compare to backtest results
└── Time-Series Cross-Validation
    ├── Multiple train-test splits
    ├── Respect temporal ordering
    └── Verify consistency

WEEK 7: STATISTICAL TESTS
├── Autocorrelation Test
│   ├── ACF near 0? (Random returns)
│   └── Ljung-Box p > 0.05?
├── Normality Test
│   ├── Jarque-Bera p > 0.05?
│   └── Shapiro-Wilk p > 0.05?
└── Stationarity Test
    ├── ADF p < 0.05?
    └── Equity curve is stable?

WEEK 7-8: COMPARISONS & REPORT
├── vs Buy-and-Hold (SPY): Alpha > 1%?
├── vs Momentum Strategy: Which wins?
├── vs Mean Reversion: Direct comparison
└── Ensemble Analysis: Can combine?

  [8] >70% parameter combinations profitable
  [9] Works on >70% of assets

FORWARD TESTING

  [11] Paper trading matches backtest
