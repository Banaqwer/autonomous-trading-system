HOW IT WORKS
============

STEP 1: Bot trades normally
  - Places entries as usual
  - Exits at target or stop

STEP 2: Log the trade
  journal.log_entry(trade_details)

STEP 3: Log the exit
  journal.log_exit(
    exit_price,
    exit_reason,        # "Target hit", "Stop loss", "Gap", etc.
    profit_loss,        # $$ amount
    market_condition,   # "Trending", "Ranging", "Volatile"
    news_event,         # "Fed announcement", "Earnings", etc.
    win_reason,         # Why did it win?
    loss_cause,         # Why did it lose?
  )

STEP 4: Analyze
  analyzer.print_analysis()

STEP 5: Learn and optimize
  - See what works best
  - Stop doing what doesn't work
  - Improve strategy


DATA COLLECTED PER TRADE
========================

Entry Details:
  - Asset, date, time, price
  - Entry pattern (which signal type)
  - Entry reason (why bot took it)
  - Stop loss and target

Exit Details:
  - Exit price, time, reason
  - Profit/loss amount and %
  - Duration held

Context:
  - Market condition (trending/ranging/volatile)
  - Volatility level (normal/spike)
  - News event (if occurred)

Analysis:
  - Why it won (pattern perfect? momentum?)
  - Why it lost (news gap? false signal?)
  - Risk/reward ratio
  - Additional notes
